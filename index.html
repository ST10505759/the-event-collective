ABOUT THE EVENT COLLECTIVE

Creating celebrations
that feel like you.

Hi, I'm Kharma De Klerk, the founder of The Event Collective.

For the past four years, I've had the privilege of creating beautiful celebrations and unforgettable memories for my clients. What began as a passion for bringing people together has grown into a career I truly love.

I believe every event should reflect the people it's celebrating. Whether it's a wedding, birthday, bridal shower, baby shower, corporate function or any special occasion, I take pride in creating experiences that are thoughtful, elegant and stress-free.

With a keen eye for detail and a genuine passion for planning, my goal is to bring your vision to life while allowing you to enjoy every moment.
